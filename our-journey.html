Our Journey

Our Story

Angie and Zack's story began with a stubborn computer password on Angie's first day of college. She didn't know why, but instead of moving to another desk and trying her luck there, she decided to turn to her left and ask for help from the guy in the Marvel shirt that matched her lunch box. They didn't mean to, but with too much in common not to go in depth on the new plot twists of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the discovery of their shared love of TTRPGs, video games, and general nerd culture interests that reached just short of speaking Klingon, they became very fast friends.

Spending nearly every break between classes walking and talking about everything under the sun, Zack fell for Angie hard and fast, even went as far as spending hundreds to get his car detailed and waiting hours for her to get out of class just to give her a ride home. Angie was much slower to realize how she felt; her family noticed it first, but it clicked for her when she realized that she was talking and thinking about her friend from school more than anyone else in her life. She very quickly broke up with her significant other at the time and confessed how she felt to Zack in the only way she knew how, by dancing around the subject very awkwardly until blurting it out while avoiding eye contact.

That was in October of 2019. They've had 7 years of movie premieres, countless game nights, escape rooms, antique malls, aquariums visited, and trips taken. They've experienced every laugh and memory made as boyfriend and girlfriend, now they would love to invite you to the start of their new adventure as Husband and Wife.

A Little About Angie

Angie is a dreamer, a creator and a lover of all things whimsical. With a paintbrush in one hand and a cup of tea in the other, she spends her days bringing stories to life through vibrant illustrations and graphic designs. Outside the studio, Angie enjoys losing herself in fantasy novels, tending to her ever‑growing collection of houseplants and wandering through vintage bookstores. She finds joy in the simple things: a good song, a warm breeze and the laughter of friends and family.

When she's not designing or reading, Angie loves exploring new cafés, trying her hand at baking and practicing yoga. Her adventurous spirit and gentle heart make every day with her an exciting new chapter.

A Little About Zack

Zack is equal parts storyteller and strategist. By day he's crafting eye-catching graphics and managing bustling online communities; by night he's a dungeon master weaving fantastical tales for friends and fellow adventurers. With a passion for graphic design and a penchant for playful creativity, Zack loves diving into new projects and learning new skills.

Beyond the screen, Zack enjoys hiking on scenic trails, experimenting with photography and spending lazy afternoons at local record shops. His infectious laughter and ability to turn any moment into an adventure make him an unforgettable companion on this journey through life.
