Our Journey

Our Story

Angie and Zack's story began with a stubborn computer password on Angie's first day of college. She didn't know why, but instead of moving to another desk and trying her luck there, she decided to turn to her left and ask for help from the guy in the Marvel shirt that matched her lunch box. They didn't mean to, but with too much in common not to go in depth on the new plot twists of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the discovery of their shared love of TTRPGs, video games, and general nerd culture interests that reached just short of speaking Klingon, they became very fast friends.

Spending nearly every break between classes walking and talking about everything under the sun, Zack fell for Angie hard and fast, even went as far as spending hundreds to get his car detailed and waiting hours for her to get out of class just to give her a ride home. Angie was much slower to realize how she felt; her family noticed it first, but it clicked for her when she realized that she was talking and thinking about her friend from school more than anyone else in her life. She very quickly confessed how she felt to Zack in the only way she knew how, by dancing around the subject very awkwardly until blurting it out while avoiding eye contact.

That was in October of 2019. They've had 7 years of movie premieres, countless game nights, escape rooms, antique malls, aquariums visited, and trips taken. They've experienced every laugh and memory made as boyfriend and girlfriend, now they would love to invite you to the start of their new adventure as Husband and Wife.

A Little About Angie

Angie is the heart behind every moment, bringing warmth, laughter, and a sense of calm wherever she goes. Whether she’s enjoying her favorite music, planning the perfect day, or diving into creative hobbies like crafting, she has a way of making even the smallest moments feel special. From rocking out to Guitar Hero to joining in on DnD adventures, Angie brings her own spark of fun and imagination into everything she does, turning ordinary days into something worth remembering.

When she’s not creating memories with those closest to her, Angie enjoys cozy days at home, spontaneous outings, and getting lost in games and creative projects alike. Her genuine kindness, bright spirit, and ability to find joy in the little things make her someone people are naturally drawn to. She’s not just a partner in life, but the kind of person who makes every moment feel effortless, meaningful, and full of love.

A Little About Zack

Zack is equal parts storyteller and strategist. By day he's crafting eye-catching graphics and managing bustling online communities; by night he's a dungeon master weaving fantastical tales for friends and fellow adventurers. With a passion for graphic design and a penchant for playful creativity, Zack loves diving into new projects and learning new skills.

Beyond the screen, Zack enjoys hiking on scenic trails, playing different games and spending lazy afternoons at local nerd shops. His infectious laughter and ability to turn any moment into an adventure make him an unforgettable companion on this journey through life.
