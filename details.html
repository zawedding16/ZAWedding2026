Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? We've probably answered it below. If not, feel free to reach out!

What is the dress code? +

We'd love for our guests to dress in cocktail / semi-formal attire. Think dresses, dress pants with a blazer, or a suit. Please DO NOT wear white or ivory, as those are reserved for the bride!

What time should I arrive? +

We recommend arriving by 3:00 PM to allow time to park and get seated comfortably before the ceremony begins at 3:30 PM. The Ceremony will be closed once the processional starts.

Is there parking at the venue? +

Yes! Free on-site parking is available at Arrowwood Weddings + Events. Just follow the signs when you arrive at 545 Loma Linda Rd, Palmer, TX 75152.

Are children welcome? +

Children are welcome! please be sure to include them in your RSVP!

Will there be an open bar? +

Yes! We will have an open bar during cocktail hour and the reception. Please drink responsibly and consider arranging a designated driver or rideshare if needed. We will also be serving our signature cocktails as well!

Can I take photos during the ceremony? +

We are having an unplugged ceremony — please keep phones and cameras put away so you can be fully present in the moment. Our photographer(s) will capture everything! Feel free to snap away during cocktail hour and the reception.

When is the RSVP deadline? +

Please RSVP by September 1, 2026. You can fill out the form on our RSVP page — it only takes a minute!

What if I have dietary restrictions? +

There is a field in the RSVP form to note any dietary restrictions or allergies. We'll do our best to accommodate you — please make sure to fill that in when you respond.
